# English reading route for the submission

All narrative material the instructor needs for the final project is available through this English route. The original Chinese requests, their exact-phrase evidence, operational prompt, frozen answer files, and historical machine reports remain checked in **unchanged** to reproduce the recorded Chinese-language evaluation. Each such source has an English reading companion below; the instructor does not need to infer meanings from untranslated rows. English translations are for reading and presentation only, **not** new test inputs or recomputed scores. Song titles, artist names, IDs, field names, hashes, and URLs are left as recorded.

Technical debugging notes, earlier prompt versions, blank forms, and `legacy_v1/` are retained as development/archive material rather than assigned reading. The [English technical-history guide](TECHNICAL_HISTORY_EN.md) explains what each important archived record means and which pre-freeze status statements are now superseded. They do not replace the selected prompt, frozen answer key, completed formal report, or English reading copies above. The freeze record in [`test_set_freeze.json`](../data/test_set_freeze.json) identifies the evaluated source hashes; the English renderings are not part of that freeze and must not be presented as if the provider processed English.
