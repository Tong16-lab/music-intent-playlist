# Author listening review of completed-run playlists

This English account translates the author's **revised** completed review on 2026-10-03. It uses saved predictions from the second formal run and the 35-track catalog; it makes no new model calls. From the 21 three-track outcomes, cases at positions 1, 6, 11, 16, and 21 in ascending case-ID order were selected: `test_001`, `test_010`, `test_017`, `test_023`, and `test_030`. The two lowest-ID refusal cases, `test_003` and `test_007`, were reviewed without listening. These are not random samples or participant ratings. “Fit” and “personal liking” are deliberately separate judgments. Timing, energy, loudness, and tonal descriptions below are listening impressions, not measured BPM, dB, or acoustic statistics.
