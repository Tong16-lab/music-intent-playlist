## V2 Decision Criteria

1. current_valence/current_arousal only describe the speaker's **current state**, and do not replace "what the first song of the playlist should be like". Events or scenarios themselves do not automatically generate current emotions; for example, "just went through a breakup" or "rainy day" are insufficient on their own to prove a specific mood. Explicit statements like "so sleepy I can't keep my eyes open" or "heart racing uncontrollably" can respectively support current low/high arousal.
2. target_valence/target_arousal describe the **musical expression** the user wants to hear. Quiet, refreshing, cheerful, sorrowful, etc., can be mapped to the corresponding dimensions; expected experiences like "healing" or "accompany me" do not automatically become precise song tags, and actual psychological effects are not guaranteed. When there is an explicit "don't be too..." style boundary that does not equal a specific tier, the original field can be recorded as `{"relation":"at_most","value":2}` or `{"relation":"at_least","value":0}`, without adding new top-level fields; existing song tags remain three-tier integers. Ranges are used only when supported by evidence and the three-tier criteria, and "don't be too" cannot be uniformly treated as a precise middle tier.
3. target_melodic_surprise continues the operational criteria already discussed in this project: broad descriptions such as "unpredictable/small surprises/variation/novelty/not too complex" are handled under the melodic exploration dimension. This mapping is merely a prior convention within this project and does not claim that general listeners will necessarily understand it this way. The mapping of "novelty" to the middle tier has been verified by the author.
   When there is an explicit request for songs to have a discernible melody, additionally populate `requires_melody_present=true`, and record the original sentence evidence in evidence_json; leave blank (null) if not expressed. This is not the seventh core intent metric and is not automatically inferred from melodic surprise preferences. When selecting songs, only treat `melody_present=yes` as satisfying the condition; both `no` and `unknown` fail to satisfy it. This tag merely indicates the presence of a trackable melody and does not guarantee that everyone can hum along.
4. trajectory is recorded as a structured object only when there is an explicit request for the **music sequence** to transition from one expression to another, such as `{"type":"from_to","arousal":{"from":3,"to":1}}`; the terminal values must equal the precise values of the target field in the same dimension. Song selection forms a target sequence of 3→2→1 based on three positions; when no qualified song is available for a position, it indicates that it cannot be fully satisfied, without using unknown tags to fake satisfaction. A user simply wanting to slowly calm down / get better on their own does not equate to requiring the first music piece to have high arousal or subsequent songs to change. When there is at least one clear music emotion/arousal target but no music sequence is specified, still use `single_target`; use `none` when there are only melody preferences, only unsupported conditions, or only soft wishes.
5. Unsupported conditions only include **song attributes** that are explicitly requested and cannot be reliably guaranteed by existing song fields, including instrumental/vocal presence, lyric language, genre, tempo, volume, cheesy/commercial pop, etc. Positive requirements (such as "electronic music") may similarly be unsupported; soft wishes like "accompany me" or "healing" do not directly become hard constraints. Words that can be explained by valence/arousal/melody tags must not be repeatedly listed under unsupported conditions.
6. For `test_024`, "not too sad", and `test_029`, "don't want to hear anything too cheerful", the author-confirmed neutral approximations are retained (where `test_029` also relies on the context of "quietly staying for a while"), and these original utterances are not singly equated to "neutral only". The author additionally confirmed that `dev_005` is operated in this project as `valence≥0`: allowing 0 and 1, and excluding -1; this may be stricter than the everyday meaning of "not too bitter/melancholic", and reports should state that this is a coarse-grained operational criterion.

## Test Sentences Verified by the Author on 2026-10-02

- test_010: "Listen to something sad first" indicates the desire to listen to such songs at this moment; "cry for a bit, and then slowly get better" describes a change in human state, **not** an arrangement instruction for three songs. Record target_valence=-1 and trajectory=single_target, with no need for first-song-specific fields, nor using this to dictate the emotions of subsequent songs.
- test_017: "Noisy/bustling" refers to high energy, not high volume; target_arousal=3.
- test_025: "Soft" describes the user's current state, not the target of the first song; current_arousal=1, target_arousal=3, and trajectory=single_target. One cannot forcibly infer first-song music requirements from "slowly... from..." in this sentence.
- test_029: Combined with "wanting to quietly stay for a while", this project accepts target_valence=0 and target_arousal=1 as an operational approximation; this does not mean that "not too cheerful" strictly equals neutral on its own.
- test_026: The author confirmed the addition of `requires_melody_present=true`, with the evidence being "tunes you can hum along to"; "no one singing" is still listed as an unsupported vocal condition. The two requirements are judged independently, and the discernible melody tag must not be used to claim that a song has no vocals.
- test_019: What the author verified is that the **song** gradually transitions from a high activity level of 3 to a low activity level of 1, with both the start and end points written in the original `trajectory` field; target_arousal=1 is the end point and is not written into current_arousal.
- test_024: "Not too boisterous" refers to a music activity upper limit of 2, rather than volume or a precise target of 2; only songs with a known activity level of 1 or 2 are allowed to satisfy this item. `target_valence=0` remains a neutral approximation confirmed by the author.

The **semantic judgments** of the above sentences have been verified by the author, and these rows in the table are labeled as ambiguous=false. The entire V2 has been verified by the author and migrated to the current official test answers, followed by the completion of freezing and evaluation.
