# English rendering of all 42 reviewed intent cases

This is a reading companion to the approved Chinese V2 table. It is **not** a replacement model input, a new answer key, or a claim that these statements came from recruited participants. The case IDs, role IDs, split, field values, and special boundaries below follow `user_intents_v2_review.tsv`. Original Chinese phrases remain the source for exact-substring evidence checks. English phrasings convey their meaning, including deliberately colloquial wording; they cannot be used to recompute the recorded Chinese-language score.

Field shorthand: `CV` current valence; `CA` current arousal; `TV` target song valence; `TA` target song arousal; `MS` target melodic surprise; `MP` explicit melody-presence requirement; `Tr` trajectory. `—` means null/not stated, not a measured zero. Valence is -1/0/1 and arousal/surprise are 1/2/3. `≥0` and `≤2` are range answers, not exact values. `single` means a music target without an explicit track-by-track path; `none` means no encoded music target/order; `3→1` means an explicit song-arousal sequence. “Unsupported” lists phrases the catalog cannot guarantee, including positive style requirements as well as exclusions. Each row was author-reviewed and approved. The accompanying notes preserve interpretive qualifications rather than silently converting soft hopes into music properties.
